# Bottom-up thrift store projection — methodology

The model builds revenue from donations, not from a top-line guess. This makes every
assumption explicit and easy to defend.

## Drivers

| Step | Formula | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| Donating households | `city households × donor market share` | Market share is the share of all households that donate to this store. |
| Annual donations | `donating households × donations per household per year` | Default 3 donations per household per year. |
| Retail revenue | `annual donations × average value per donation` | The resale value generated by one donation. Sets the top line. |
| Customers | `retail revenue ÷ average transaction value` | Average transaction value is the average customer basket. |
| Salvage sales | `retail revenue × salvage %` | Recycled/textile salvage income. Default 0.8%. |
| Total revenue | `retail + salvage` | |

Year 2 donor market share is the **midpoint** of Year 1 and Year 3, so the user only sets the
two endpoints.

## Expense line items (kept identical every run)

| Line | Driver | Default |
|------|--------|---------|
| Credit card fees | % of total revenue | 2.0% |
| Insurance liability | fixed annual $ | $2,000 |
| Internet, Phones | fixed annual $ | $3,000 |
| Payroll | labor % of total revenue | 60% |
| Payroll taxes | % of payroll | 7.5% |
| Pest control | fixed annual $ | $300 |
| Rent | sq ft × rent per sq ft | local market rate |
| Repairs/maintenance | fixed annual $ | $1,600 |
| Security system | fixed annual $ | $900 |
| Store/office supplies | fixed annual $ | $3,700 |
| Utilities/trash service | fixed annual $ | $22,300 |

`Total expenses = sum of the lines above`. `Net profit = total revenue − total expenses`.
`Net margin = net profit ÷ total revenue`.

## One-time startup costs (Year 0, capital — not in operating expenses)

| Line | Default |
|------|---------|
| Thrift Store Consultant | $100,000 |
| Equipment & Fixtures | $45,000 |
| Lease Deposit + First Month Rent | `rent ÷ 12 × 3` (3 months) |
| Build-out / Renovations / Tenant Improvements | $50,000 |
| Legal, Permits, Insurance Setup, Signage | $13,500 |

`Simple payback (years) = total startup ÷ Year 1 net profit`.

## Calibration reference (Longview, TX, 10k sq ft store, 2013 first full year actuals)

- ~6,900 donations in the first full year → ~2,300 donating households → about a **7% donor
  market share** against ~31,800 city households.
- Retail sales ~$573K → about **$83 of resale value per donation**.
- Average basket ~$15–$20 in the local actuals.

Use these as a reality check. If a requested market share implies donations far above a city's
plausible donor base, raise it with the user before finalizing.
